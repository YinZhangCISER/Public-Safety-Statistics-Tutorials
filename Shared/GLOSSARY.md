# Glossary

*Developed by Yin Zhang, PhD, Assistant Professor, Data Analytics Program, Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Washington State University, for the Washington Data Exchange for Public Safety (WADEPS) through the Center for Interdisciplinary Statistical Education and Research (CISER).*

Plain language definitions for every term used in this repository. Terms are added as each level is published, so a term you cannot find here has not been introduced yet. The module where a term is first explained is given in the last column.

## Time series, Beginner level

| Term | In plain language | First used |
|---|---|---|
| Aggregation | Grouping smaller time units into larger ones, for example days into months, by adding or averaging them. | Topic 4 |
| Autocorrelation | The extent to which a value is related to its own earlier values. High autocorrelation means the data has memory. | Topic 16 |
| Count | The raw number of events in a period, for example 25 use of force incidents in July. | Topic 5 |
| Cross sectional data | A snapshot of many units at a single point in time. A photograph rather than a movie. | Topic 2 |
| Cycle | A pattern that repeats over a long and irregular period, typically three to ten years, such as an economic downturn. | Topic 9 |
| Frequency | How often the data is recorded: hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly. | Topic 4 |
| Lag | The delay between an action, such as a policy change, and its visible effect in the data. | Topic 15 |
| Moving average | A smoothing method that replaces each value with the average of itself and its neighbours. | Topic 13 |
| Noise | Random fluctuation with no specific cause. What is left after trend, season and cycle are removed. | Topic 10 |
| Non stationary | A series whose average or variability changes over time, because it has a trend, a season, or both. | Topic 17 |
| Outlier | A value so far outside the normal range that it most likely reflects a one off event rather than a change in the underlying pattern. | Topic 11 |
| Provisional data | Recent months whose records are still being entered, so the counts will rise later. | Topic 19 |
| Rate | A count expressed against an activity baseline, for example use of force divided by arrests. | Topic 5 |
| Reporting bias | Distortion caused by changes in recording systems, definitions, or staff behaviour rather than by changes in the world. | Topic 19 |
| Seasonality | A pattern that repeats at a fixed and predictable interval, most often every twelve months. | Topic 8 |
| Smoothing | Any method that reduces noise in order to make the underlying signal visible. | Topic 13 |
| Stationarity | The property of a series whose average and variability stay constant over time. | Topic 17 |
| Time series | A sequence of values recorded at successive, equally spaced points in time. | Topic 1 |
| Trend | The long term direction of a series, ignoring short term movement. | Topic 7 |
| Year over year | A comparison of a period to the same period in the previous year, which removes the seasonal effect. | Topic 18 |

## WADEPS terms

| Term | In plain language |
|---|---|
| CAD | Computer Aided Dispatch. The software a dispatch centre uses to log and assign calls. It is what produces call for service data. |
| CFS | Calls for Service. A record of each event a police response was requested for, or that an officer initiated and reported. |
| Incident type | The overall nature of the problem behind a call. WADEPS uses eight of them. |
| Incident detail | The specific circumstance behind a call. WADEPS uses 32 of them, each paired with a parent incident type. |
| PSAP | Primary Safety Answering Point. The dispatch centre that receives emergency calls. Washington has 78 PSAP partnership groups. |
| Reportable use of force | A use of force meeting the state threshold for mandatory reporting to WADEPS. Narrower than any use of force at all. |
| Sworn officer | A commissioned law enforcement officer, as distinct from civilian agency staff. |
