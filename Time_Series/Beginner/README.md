# Time Series, Beginner Level

### Understanding time based data, with no statistics background

*Prepared by Yin Zhang, Center for Interdisciplinary Statistical Education and Research (CISER), Washington State University.*

**No statistics. No math. No code.** Twenty self contained modules, about five minutes each. All twenty are published. Read them in order for a complete foundation, or jump to the one that answers the question in front of you.

## What you will be able to do

By the end of Part I you can say what a time series is, tell it apart from a snapshot, and see how the choice of time unit and denominator changes the answer.

By the end of Part II you can look at any chart of incidents over time and separate trend from season, season from cycle, a real change from noise, and a real spike from a data problem.

**Next:** the [Intermediate level](../Intermediate/) builds these series yourself, from records, in code.
